# sleep-supplement-research-guide
Sleep Supplement Guide - UK
UK Sleep and Supplement Research Resources

This repository collects educational resources for people researching sleep, nutrition, supplements and healthy ageing in the UK.

The aim is to make it easier to find reliable information, understand the limitations of supplement research and make informed decisions.

Topics covered

Magnesium and sleep

L-theanine and relaxation

Omega-3 fatty acids

Creatine and healthy ageing

Sleep habits and sleep hygiene

Supplement labels, dosage and safety

Research and safety

Use reputable sources such as the NHS, relevant UK government guidance and peer-reviewed scientific literature when evaluating health claims.

Supplement evidence varies by ingredient and intended use. This repository is for general educational purposes only and does not provide medical advice.

Contributions

Suggestions for reliable research resources and corrections are welcome. Please include a source for factual claims.
